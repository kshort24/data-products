Declared limits

  • Causation is not identified anywhere in this product. No coaching, medical, or intervention log exists in this data plane. Persona actions are testable hypotheses mapped to remit.
  • Batting order is not a column here — lineup-slot questions are out of scope.
  • The recent window is 129 PA / five weeks. Sub-splits inside it fall below the 50-PA floor fast and carry ⚠ everywhere.
  • D-7 / O-13, found by this build's own verification harness: the governed chase_rate_g derives in_zone_rate by subtraction, so NULL-zone rows are silently counted in-zone. Disclosed, not patched; every zone rate here uses in_zone_rate_fix.
  • AD-1 and ST-1 are NEW-PROVISIONAL and need DPO ratification before reuse.
  • xwOBAcon ≠ xwOBA (O-4). Shifts are compared; levels are never cross-compared to wOBA.
