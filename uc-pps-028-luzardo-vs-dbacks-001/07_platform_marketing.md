## 2 · Reusable patterns this UC contributes

| Pattern | Generalises to |
|---|---|
| **G8 — a superlative is not a finding until its metric and cohort are named** | Every "best / most / worst X" ask, in any domain. The single most portable thing in this package. |
| **G9 — never publish a composite index for a contested claim** | Any scorecard, vendor ranking, or health index where the client already believes an answer. Publish the axes; let them disagree in public. |
| **CN-1…CN-6 — variance is not level** | Any reliability question about a repeated process: SLA adherence, delivery predictability, forecast stability, supplier consistency. The atom (`start_frame`) is just "one row per execution of the repeated thing." |
| **The parent-reproduction check** | Every product that claims to *extend* an earlier one. If it cannot reproduce the parent's published figures, it is a contradiction, not an extension. Cheap to run, and it caught nothing here — which is the point. |
| **AR-1 — recency tiering on entity-vs-*group* history** | Any "our history with <company / team / segment>" panel, where the group persists and its members turn over. |
| **D-1 — completeness is grain-relative** | Every DQ suite anywhere. A null-rate check without a declared grain is a coin flip. |
| **A sensitivity scan is a filter over a precomputed atom** | Any parameter sweep. The naive nesting is quadratic and will time out. |

## 3 · Publication notes

**Two dashboard builds from one payload.** The offline copy vendors nothing — no CDN, no webfont, no
external asset — per the `uc-pos-011` rule, so it opens from a thumb drive in a clubhouse with no
network. The published copy adds a Google-hosted webfont pairing (Barlow Condensed / Source Sans 3 /
IBM Plex Mono) that the offline copy cannot embed without carrying font binaries. **The data is
byte-identical; only the type differs**, and both are generated by the same script from the same
`payload.json`.

**Categorical palette** `#8250C4 · #E81828 · #00919E · #C97A00` — validated with the six-checks
validator in **both light and dark**: lightness band, chroma floor, CVD separation, normal-vision
floor and surface contrast all PASS. The repo's previous pitch palette failed two checks
(`#8C564B` reads as gray and is confusable with `#E81828` under protanopia, ΔE 4.5) — **a candidate
correction for the shared brand guidelines**, not just this build.

**Privacy.** Public Statcast telemetry only. Safe to share; the hosted page is private until the
owner shares it.

## 5 · One-paragraph summary for a wider audience

> A data product organisation was asked to pre-scout a pitcher and was handed a premise: *"maybe the
> most consistent pitcher on the staff."* Rather than describe the premise, the design was built to
> break it — "consistency" was split into six independently ranked axes, and the window boundary the
> client chose was scanned across eight alternatives. The result is a split verdict: the pitcher is
> unambiguously the staff's best by contact quality at every possible cut of the season, but the
> consistency title belongs to a teammate on the axes that describe a floor, and his own #1 ranking
> in start-to-start variation exists only if you cut the year exactly where the premise cut it. The
> product reports both, names the boundary, and hands over the receipts.
