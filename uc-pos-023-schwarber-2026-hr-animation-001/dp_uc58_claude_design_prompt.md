# Claude Design brief: "Forty-Five and a Question Mark"
### Kyle Schwarber's 2026 home runs, animated · UC #58 · `uc-pos-023` · `dp_uc58`

> **For Claude Design.** You are animating a data story that a video editor will splice with Baseball Savant home-run clips. Every number you put on screen is in the DATA block at the end; use those values exactly and invent none. Where this brief and your taste disagree on data, the brief wins; on motion and type, use your judgment inside the brand tokens.

## 0 · What to make
- A **motion piece, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps**, dark field, ≈ 95 s of animation in total, **delivered as 16 numbered segments** (`seg01` … `seg16`, table in §5). Each segment that ends in **CUT** ends on a clean frame the editor cuts away from; the next segment starts on the matching zoomed-in frame and zooms out (§3).
- If you can export video, export each segment as its own MP4 (H.264). If you can only produce an animated page, build one page with a segment selector and a fixed 1920 × 1080 stage so each segment can be screen-captured frame-accurately.
- Reference end-state frames for every scene are attached: `dp_uc58_k1_pre_phillies.png`, `dp_uc58_k2_phillies_tenure.png`, `dp_uc58_k3_2026_stops.png`, `dp_uc58_k4_season_table.png`, `dp_uc58_k5_spray.png`, `dp_uc58_k6_pitchmap_lhp.png`, `dp_uc58_k7_pitchmap_rhp.png`, `dp_uc58_k8_consolidated.png`. Match their data, geometry and colors; restyle freely.

## 1 · Non-negotiables (data fidelity)
1. **Step lines, not smooth lines.** Home runs are counts: each line is flat until the next homer, then steps up by one. X = plate appearances into that season (`hr_at_pa` lists the PA on which each homer came).
2. **X is plate appearances, not pitches.** Axis title: "Plate appearances into the season". Range 0–757; Y 0–60.
3. **One line per season, 12 seasons.** 2016 is two games and zero homers. Draw it and label it; don't drop it. 2021 is one line (Nationals + Red Sox).
4. **Regular season only** in every line, table, dot and count. The Wild Card homer appears only where §4 says, always labelled "postseason".
5. **Spray chart honesty.** All 45 homers, from every park, are drawn on one Citizens Bank Park outline. Show the caption "Every park, drawn on the CBP outline". Never say or imply a road homer "would have been out" in Philadelphia. The two dots marked `approx: true` are **hollow**, with a footnote: "2 homers have no hit coordinates; placed by the field named in the play description".
6. **Pitch maps are the catcher's view.** Draw the zone rectangle from `zone` (half-width 0.83 ft, 1.61–3.18 ft). Draw each ball **to scale** (0.242 ft across), never sized by velocity.
7. **Colors carry meaning in one scene at a time.** Era colors (S1–S4), pitcher-hand colors (S5), pitch-group colors (S6–S7). S8 shows all three, each panel with its own key.
8. Pitcher names appear only where the DATA gives one. Otherwise caption with date, opponent, exit velocity and distance.

## 3 · Transition grammar
- **T1 · Cut-out (into a clip).** Hold the highlighted mark for 12 frames with a red ring pulse. Then push in ≈ 4× over 18 frames, centred on the mark, while the caption card slides up. End on a 6-frame wipe in surface color. **The segment ends there (CUT).**
- **T2 · Return (from a clip).** Start on the same zoomed frame and the same mark. Zoom out over 24 frames (ease-out) to the full view, then continue.
- **T3 · Labels to table (S4).** When the 2026 line lands at 45, hold 1 s. Then each season's end label flies into its row of the ranked table, sorted by HR, while the lines dim to 30%. The 2026 row ends on a red plate.
- **T4 · Consolidation (S8).** The three screens shrink into a 3-panel grid: cumulative left, spray centre, pitch maps stacked right (LHP over RHP). Use a 15-frame stagger.

**S8 · All three (≈ 8 s).** Apply T4. Title: **"Forty-five and a question mark"**. Subtitle: "45 HR · tied for the MLB lead · shorter, softer, more pulled · 30 off righties, 15 off lefties". Hold 3 s, then fade to surface.
