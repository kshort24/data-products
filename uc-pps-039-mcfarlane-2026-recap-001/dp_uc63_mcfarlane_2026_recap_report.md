## Caveats
- Small sample. 85 PA and 333 pitches; every stand × pitch cell is under 50 swings. Read rates as directional.
- Client frame vs governed context. The client's tables include the 2 Wild Card games (as his `pps` does); context percentiles use his 313 regular-season pitches against regular-season pools.
- One automatic ball (9/27, no pitch thrown) is inside the 333 and counts as in-zone in the inherited chase function (AB-1, D-7). It moves the vs-LHB usage shares to a 99.4% total; no headline moves.
- FO-1 is raw velocity and spin, not drift-adjusted (UC #47 drift-adjusts; the order does not change at the top). Opponent pitchers in the pool are identified by MLBAM id only (EN-3: in batting rows `player_name` is the hitter).
- AAA is a supporting tier (counts, usage, velocity, whiff), never blended into MLB rates; AA has no Statcast in the plane.
- Carry-ins (draft, recalls, Thomson dismissal, AA line) are quoted from public sources and never computed on.
- Postseason: 2 Wild Card outings vs Atlanta, 6 PA. Too few to read.
