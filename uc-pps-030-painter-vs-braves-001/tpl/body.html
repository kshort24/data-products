How to read the grades. The 20-80 scale is a z-score in costume: grade = clip(50 + 10 × z, 20, 80), rounded to the nearest 5, against a declared population — every right-handed pitcher who threw in a Phillies regular-season game 2015–2026 with ≥100 pitches of that type in that season. This is a Phillies-schedule population, not a league population; a 60 here is not a Savant 60. Shape (velocity, ride, run) is reported but deliberately not scored into the pitch grade — a pitch is worth what it misses and where it lands.
