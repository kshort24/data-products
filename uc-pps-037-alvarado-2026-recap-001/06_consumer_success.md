## 4 · How to read DB-1
- Each row is one signature that a declining or an unlucky reliever would show, with a bar written before the 2026 numbers were read.
- The bar chart shows how far 2026 moved in the predicted direction, measured in bars: PRESENT at ≥ 1, CONTRA at ≤ −1.
- Decline is SUPPORTED when a majority of its six signatures are PRESENT and none is CONTRA. Blip works the same way over its two.
- **It does not** predict 2027, price a contract, or see anything the log cannot (health, mechanics video, the 2025 suspension's effects).

## 5 · FAQ
- **Why "runs on watch" and not ERA?** Statcast has no earned-run flag. The 5.86 ERA is quoted as a carry-in (NBC).
- **Is "#14 of 199" MLB-wide?** No. It counts Phillies pitchers 2015–26, regular season. "#3 among relievers" uses RP-1t (starts ≤ 20% of games).
- **Why do your notebook's numbers differ a little?** Your `pps` frame includes spring and postseason pitches (388 in the tenure). The governed frame is regular season only.
- **Why does the cumulative chart end lower than yours?** CM-D1: the house module's wOBA counts intentional walks and sac bunts in the numerator. Your 2026 line ended at .360; the same-basis value is .351.
- **Why two zone rates?** The 2026 log includes 16 automatic balls and strikes with no pitch (AB-1). The house rate counts them in-zone (42.2%); counting only pitches thrown gives 41.4%.
