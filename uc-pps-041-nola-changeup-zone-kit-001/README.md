## The answer in six lines
1. The 28 clips reconcile exactly: 27 regular-season + 1 Wild Card changeups taken for strikes in zones 7–9 (25 vs LHB).
2. None came with two strikes: 9 on the first pitch, 15 with Nola behind.
3. Drawn at true baseball size, all 28 touch the bottom row; 8 are centered just under it (the ball only has to clip the zone).
4. 23.1% of his 0-0 / 1-1 pitches to lefties were changeups, 2nd of 30 Phillies starter seasons since 2015 (behind his own 2023); 13.0% at two strikes.
5. 48% of his changeups finished below the zone; when one landed in the bottom third, 23% were taken for strikes (5th of 30).
6. The catch: 22.1% of first pitches to lefties were changeups (ties his high), only 40% of them strikes (career low).
