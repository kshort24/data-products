# Forty-Five and a Question Mark
### Kyle Schwarber · 2026 season recap · UC #60 · `uc-pos-024` · `dp_uc60` v1.0.0 · Phillies Offense

> Kyle Schwarber's 2026: 45 home runs, tied for the MLB lead, and a .367 wOBA that ranks in the 90th percentile of Phillies hitter seasons. Then a second half that made everyone ask about his back.

## 1 · Bottom line
1. **A good year, not a lost one.** .232/.354/.502, wOBA .367, xwOBA .366. That is his second-best Phillies season by wOBA, and well above the median Phillies 600-PA season (.330 xwOBA).
2. **It turned in July.** First half wOBA .394 on 415 PA; second half .329 on 287. July was the real hole (xwOBA .303). By August and September his expected line (.352, .364) had come back faster than the results.
3. **The back is not visible in the log.** Bat speed before the 6/23 scratch 74.1 mph, after 74.4. The biggest xwOBA split lands on 5/16, 33 games before it. That does not rule an injury out; it says the swing never measurably slowed.
4. **The pop thinned, it didn't vanish.** Barrel rate 15.5%, a hair under his tenure low, but only 10.6% after the break (first half 19.3%). EV p90 108.6, ISO .270 and HR rate all stayed inside his Phillies range.
5. **One real flag: chase.** 26.1% vs 21.4% over 2022–25 (z 4.1), his highest since 2020. Strikeouts rose less (31.3%, z 1.2). And of the cells with enough PA, his damage on righty fastballs fell hardest.

## 6 · Should we be concerned?
**Graded: NOT SUPPORTED** on the pre-registered bar (CN-1). His xwOBA (.366) sits above his own 2022–25 low and well above the Phillies ≥ 600-PA median. The process leg needs both strikeouts and chase to move beyond noise; only chase did (z 4.1; strikeouts z 1.2).

![](out/dp_uc60_f7_k_vs_chase.png)
*Across 117 other Phillies hitter seasons, chase explains almost none of strikeout rate (R² 0.00). Every season of his sits above the line, so his strikeouts come from whiffs, not from chasing. That makes 2026's chase jump a separate problem to fix.*

## 7 · Three things for 2027
1. **Chase, first.** Bring it back toward his 2022–25 21.4%. It is the only process number that moved beyond noise.
2. **Righty fastballs.** xwOBA on them fell from .495 to .386 (205 PA). That is where the 2025 version of him lived.
3. **The damage band.** 16.4% of his tracked contact landed at 20–32°, after 18.1% in 2025. It is the launch-angle drift UC33 flagged at midseason, and it never fully came back.
